1. Tomatina tickets — check right now
The Tomatina + Bus from Valencia package is €45. Tickets historically sell out between March–June. Since today is May 26, they may already be gone or nearly sold out. Check tomatina.es/en immediately. If sold out, try latomatinatours.com or PP Travel (from £129).
2. Schengen visa — apply this week
You can apply up to 3 months before travel. Processing is 15–25 working days (can extend to 45–60 days in peak season). If you're flying Aug 20, apply no later than June 10 to stay safe. Apply via BLS International (india.blsspainvisa.com). Cost: ₹8,920 embassy fee + ₹1,675 service = ₹10,595–₹10,847. If based in Bengaluru, the BLS centre is in Bengaluru — no need to travel to Delhi.
3. Flights — book within the week
August is peak season. DEL → BCN roundtrip is currently ₹55,000–₹68,000 (1 stop via Qatar, Etihad, or Lufthansa). Every day of delay = higher prices. Best route: Delhi → Barcelona (arrive), Madrid → Delhi (return). Search on Google Flights or Skyscanner. Enable price alerts now.
4. Hostel pre-booking for Valencia
Valencia hostels around Tomatina week (Aug 24–28) fill up fast. Book your Valencia hostel on Hostelworld ASAP. Barcelona and Madrid are less time-sensitive but book at least 6 weeks out.

Barcelona tourist tax — 2026 increase

Biggest savings levers
Book flights today. August prices for India-Europe are rising daily. Every week you wait could add ₹3,000–8,000 to the fare. Set a Google Flights price alert right now at ₹58,000 RT.
Hostel kitchen = cut food costs by 40%. Breakfast and one meal a day from the hostel kitchen easily saves €8–10/day. Supermarkets: Mercadona, Lidl, Carrefour.
Free attractions are excellent. Barcelona Cathedral, Gothic Quarter, Barceloneta beach, La Boqueria (browse free), Valencia's Turia Gardens, Retiro Park Madrid, walking neighbourhoods — easily fills 3 days at €0.
Prado Museum free on Tuesday 6–8pm. Worth timing your Madrid day around this. City of Arts and Sciences in Valencia has cheaper combo passes — check cac.es for current options.
Menú del día = best value eat. Spanish lunch set menus (menú del día, €10–14) typically include 3 courses + drink. Order lunch as your main meal; dinner can be tapas or hostel cooked food.
Train over flight for internal travel. Barcelona→Valencia by train (€25–35 booked early) beats flying once you add airport time. Same for Valencia→Madrid AVE. Book on Renfe at least 3–4 weeks out.
Spanish SIM at BCN airport. Orange, Vodafone, or Lebara SIM (~€15) covers all of Spain with 15–20GB data. Much cheaper than roaming. Alternatively, buy an EU eSIM on Airalo before departure.
Tomatina clothes from home, not Spain. Bring old clothes from India (₹100–200 from local market) for the tomato fight. Don't buy them in Spain — far more expensive. Bring a cheap pair of sandals too.
Download offline maps before you go. Google Maps offline packs for Barcelona, Valencia, and Madrid. Also download the Spanish phrasebook in Google Translate for offline use.
