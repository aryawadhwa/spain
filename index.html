12-Night Multi-City Route Map

A balanced travel itinerary starting from Barcelona, moving to Valencia for the festival, and concluding in Madrid.

Tue, Aug 18
Depart India Flight
Board afternoon flight from Delhi (DEL 15:55) with Qatar Airways. Transit through Doha (DOH). Enjoy a smooth transition to Europe.
Wed, Aug 19
Arrive Barcelona
Land at BCN Airport at 07:30. Take Metro L9 Sud straight to your central hostel. Recover, take a slow afternoon stroll in Gothic Quarter.
Thu, Aug 20
Barcelona — Gaudí Architecture
Explore La Sagrada Família (pre-booked ticket essential, €26) and Park Güell. Have a tapas lunch at Mercat de la Boqueria.
Mon, Aug 24
Barcelona → Valencia Train
Board modern Renfe train (~3.5 hr, €25). Arrive in Valencia in the afternoon. Check in to your social hostel. Walk along the lush Turia Riverbed gardens.
Wed, Aug 26
LA TOMATINA FESTIVAL Buñol
The Big Event! Board official early coach from Valencia (07:00). Join the crowds at Plaza del Pueblo. Greased pole challenge at 10:00. Tomato trucks roll in at 12:00. Shower up, return in afternoon, and hit the official Afterparty.
Sat, Aug 29
Valencia → Madrid Train
Take the high-speed AVE train (1.5 hr, €20) directly to Madrid Atocha station. Spend the evening along historic Gran Vía and Plaza Mayor.
Sun, Aug 30
Depart Madrid → India Flight
Head to Madrid-Barajas Airport (MAD) for your evening return flight (MAD 21:50) with Etihad Airways. Transit through Abu Dhabi (AUH).
Mon, Aug 31
Arrive Delhi
Land at Delhi (DEL) at 14:10. Home sweet home! Unpack, recover, and reminisce about the ultimate tomato food fight in Buñol!
